Women's Safety Tips

Small safety habits can make a big difference. Learn how to stay prepared in different situations.

1. Personal Safety

Personal safety means taking care of yourself and being aware of the people and places around you. Always trust your instincts and avoid situations that make you uncomfortable.

  • Stay aware of your surroundings.
  • Keep emergency contacts accessible.
  • Know where to ask for help.
  • Move towards a public place if you feel unsafe.

2. Travel Safety

Travelling safely requires planning and awareness. Before starting your journey, check your route and keep your phone charged.

  • Use trusted transport services.
  • Share your trip details with someone you trust.
  • Avoid isolated places whenever possible.
  • Keep important contacts saved on your phone.

3. Digital Safety

Digital safety helps protect your personal information while using the internet and social media platforms.

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Never share OTPs or banking details.
  • Enable two-step verification.
  • Report suspicious online activities.

4. Self Defence Awareness

Learning basic self-defence techniques from a qualified instructor can improve confidence. Focus on creating distance from danger, reaching a safer place and contacting help.
